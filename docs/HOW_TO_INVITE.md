# How to Invite Someone to a Meeting

## 🎯 Quick Guide

There are **two easy ways** to invite someone to your video meeting:

---

## **Method 1: Share the Invite Link (Recommended)**

1. **Start or join a meeting**
2. **Click the "Invite Link" button** at the top of the screen
   - It's the blue button with a copy icon
3. **The link is now copied!** You'll see "Link Copied!" confirmation
4. **Paste and share** the link via:
   - Email
   - Slack/Teams
   - WhatsApp
   - Any messaging app

**Example link:**
```
http://localhost:5173/dashboard/meet/mpz6i1dn
```

When they click the link, they'll be taken directly to the meeting!

---

## **Method 2: Share the Meeting ID**

1. **Look at the meeting header** - you'll see something like `Work Meeting: mpz6i1dn`
2. **Copy the ID** (e.g., `mpz6i1dn`)
3. **Share the ID** with your teammate
4. They go to **Dashboard → Meetings**
5. They click **"Join Existing"**
6. They enter the meeting ID
7. They click **"Join Session"**

---

## **Best Practices**

### ✅ **Do:**
- Share the full invite link for the easiest experience
- Test your camera and mic before inviting others
- Send the link a few minutes before the meeting starts
- Keep the meeting ID handy in case someone needs it

### ❌ **Don't:**
- Share meeting links publicly (they're not password-protected yet)
- Forget to check your camera/mic permissions
- Close the meeting tab if you're the host

---

## **Troubleshooting**

### **"They can't join the meeting"**
- Make sure they're logged into CollabSuite
- Verify they're using the correct link or ID
- Check if their browser allows camera/microphone access

### **"The link doesn't work"**
- Ensure the meeting is still active
- Try sharing the Meeting ID instead
- Have them manually navigate to Meetings → Join Existing

### **"I can't copy the link"**
- Your browser might be blocking clipboard access
- Manually copy the URL from your browser's address bar
- Or share just the Meeting ID

---

## **Technical Details**

- **Meeting IDs** are unique 8-character codes (e.g., `x2k8j3n9`)
- **Links** are automatically generated: `[YOUR_DOMAIN]/dashboard/meet/[MEETING_ID]`
- **No limit** on the number of participants (depends on your network)
- **Peer-to-peer** connection using WebRTC
